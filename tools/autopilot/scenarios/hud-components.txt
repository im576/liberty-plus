# T-049 research spike (rule 4: do not guess engine behaviour). Which vanilla HUD elements can be hidden on their own, without the radar?
# The engine lists every hud.dat component it found (hud_component lines: name, live position/size/alpha, and the globals), then hides
# groups of them one at a time so the screenshots show what each one is. Nothing here is a pass/fail on the game's look: the output is
# the evidence for docs/research/HudComponents.md and for vanillaComponents in config/hud.json. Names the game does not have reply
# "not found" and the run goes on. Every hide is restored before the next.
await-alive 60000
expect "autopilot_player_ready" 65
god on
wanted 0
goto east_park
expect "teleport_done id=east_park" 90
wait 4000
time 13 0
weather 1
hudctl force all off
hudctl probe-mode on
hudctl table
expect "hud_table components=\d+" 10
select 0
catalog give service-pistol 150 clear
select 7
wait 1500
wanted 3
wait 1500
shot hud_vanilla_baseline

# Weapon icon and ammunition.
hudctl hide HUD_WEAPON_ICON
wait 800
shot hud_hidden_weapon_only
hudctl restore
hudctl hide HUD_AMMO
wait 800
shot hud_hidden_ammo_only
hudctl restore
hudctl hide HUD_WEAPON_ICON
hudctl hide HUD_AMMO
wait 800
shot hud_hidden_weapon_ammo
hudctl restore
# Wanted stars.
hudctl hide HUD_WANTED_BACK
hudctl hide HUD_WANTED_FRONT
wait 800
shot hud_hidden_wanted
hudctl restore
# Cash.
hudctl hide-matching CASH
wait 800
shot hud_hidden_cash
hudctl restore
# The radar and whatever carries the player's health and armour arcs.
hudctl hide-matching RADAR
wait 800
shot hud_hidden_radar
hudctl restore
hudctl hide-matching HEALTH
wait 800
shot hud_hidden_health
hudctl restore
hudctl hide-matching ARMOUR
hudctl hide-matching ARMOR
wait 800
shot hud_hidden_armour
hudctl restore
# Everything the table has, to see what stays (text, help messages, mission and street names).
hudctl hide-matching HUD_
wait 800
shot hud_hidden_everything_in_table
hudctl restore
wait 800
shot hud_restored
hudctl probe-mode off
hudctl status
expect "hud_status enabled=True" 10
wanted 0
